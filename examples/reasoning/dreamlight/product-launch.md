# Case: Premium AI Product Launch

## User Request

Create a premium SVG hero visual for an AI product launch.

## Design Analysis

Goal:

- futuristic but trustworthy
- premium rather than flashy
- communicate intelligence and calm

Avoid:

- random neon effects
- excessive particles
- generic AI gradients

## System Selection

Archetype:
Dreamlight

Palette:
Night

Layout:
Hero

## Composition Decision

Use:

- one dominant visual gesture
- large negative space
- one clear focal point

The hero should communicate one idea, not many decorations.

## Material Decision

Use controlled light and glass depth.

Glass is used for hierarchy, not decoration.

## Typography Decision

Hierarchy:

- Display: 72px
- Title: 48px
- Body: 18px

## Motion Decision

Ambient light movement only.

Purpose:
show atmosphere and material response.

## Anti-AI Review

Reject if:

- more than one competing light source
- random floating spheres
- excessive glow
- template card composition
